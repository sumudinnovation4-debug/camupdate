Install the app Recommended

Works on Android and iPhone. Opens full-screen from your home screen and updates itself.

Android — how to install
  1. Open camplugie.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
iPhone — how to install
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android APK

Prefer a regular Android app file? Download and open it. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.
