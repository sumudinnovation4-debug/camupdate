About GLCGF

Founded on faith, run on accountability, based in Keffi.

Who we are

GLC Global Foundation (GLCGF) is a people-centred, non-profit and humanitarian organization committed to improving the lives of individuals, families and communities through practical, sustainable and impactful interventions.

The Foundation seeks to respond to pressing social and humanitarian needs through practical interventions in education, healthcare, poverty alleviation, community development, empowerment and humanitarian assistance.

Our purpose

Empowering individuals and communities through education, healthcare, humanitarian assistance, youth development, community interventions and support for vulnerable persons.

GLCGF identifies social, educational, health, economic and humanitarian challenges affecting vulnerable and underserved populations and develops programmes that provide meaningful support, opportunity and hope.

Our history & divine foundation

A Foundation Born from a Vision to Serve Humanity

GLC Global Foundation (GLCGF) was established from a divine vision and a deep conviction that the love of God should be demonstrated through practical service to humanity. The Foundation was born out of a desire to reach vulnerable people, restore hope, protect children, and create opportunities for individuals and families facing difficult circumstances.

Our journey is rooted in the belief that every human being has dignity, value, and potential. We believe that compassion must go beyond words; it must be expressed through action, care, support, education, empowerment, and opportunities that can positively transform lives.

From the beginning, our vision has been particularly concerned with children who are vulnerable, disadvantaged, neglected, or unable to access quality education. We recognize that poverty, family hardship, lack of educational resources, difficult living conditions, and other challenges can prevent children from reaching their full potential.

Vision & mission

Vision

A transformed society where every individual has access to opportunity, dignity, education, healthcare and the support needed to achieve a better and more meaningful life.

Mission

To reach vulnerable and underserved people with practical expressions of God’s love through educational opportunities, healthcare support, humanitarian assistance, empowerment and community development initiatives.

Our core values

Love & compassion

Dignity, care and understanding.

Integrity

Honest and ethical service.

Accountability

Responsible stewardship of resources and results.

Transparency

Open communication and responsible reporting.

Empowerment

Creating pathways to self-reliance.

Inclusion

Ensuring vulnerable groups are not left behind.

Our objectives

  • Promote access to quality education for disadvantaged and vulnerable children and young people.
  • Provide scholarships, school-fee assistance, examination support and educational materials.
  • Promote access to basic healthcare through outreach, screening, health education and referral support.
  • Support vulnerable persons, disadvantaged families and underserved communities.
  • Promote youth empowerment, skills development, entrepreneurship and economic opportunities.
  • Implement community development initiatives based on locally identified needs.
  • Provide humanitarian assistance during emergencies and periods of heightened vulnerability.
  • Promote social inclusion, dignity and equal opportunities.
  • Mobilize resources and establish partnerships for sustainable community development.
  • Support initiatives that contribute to healthier, better educated, empowered and resilient communities.

Where we work

Keffi

Our secretariat and headquarters, in Nasarawa State, Nigeria.

Bagaji

Home of Phase 1 of Operation Education, with 90 pupils supported at Bagaji Primary School.

Saura

Site of a community medical outreach with health checks and basic medicine.

Milestones so far

  • 7 May 2026: GLCGF founded
  • 20 July 2026: 90 pupils supported with examination fees at Bagaji Primary School
  • 14 August 2026: 200 community members reached at the Saura medical outreach
  • 14 September 2026: 100 pupils reached at Bagaji Primary School, with 25 children supported through a complete street-to-school transition
  • 24 October 2026: Medical outreach (upcoming)
