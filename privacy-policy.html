Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026 · Applies to the Camplugie website, Android app, and APK.
Overview

Camplugie ("we", "us", "our") is a campus marketplace and community app. This policy explains what information we collect when you use Camplugie, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Account & profile

  • Name, email, and profile photo from Google Sign-In (we use Google-only authentication)
  • University / school you select or are affiliated with
  • Username, bio, and any other profile details you choose to add

Marketplace & transactions

  • Listings you create (title, price, photos, description, category)
  • Order, escrow, and payment records (amount, status, buyer/seller, timestamps) processed via Paystack
  • Bank account details you add for payouts/withdrawals (collected and tokenized by Paystack — Camplugie does not store full card or bank credentials)
  • Wallet balance and transaction history

Chat, social & groups

  • Messages, media (photos, videos, voice notes, documents), and reactions you send in 1:1 or group chats
  • Posts, comments, likes, reposts, and other Yard feed activity
  • Reports you file against other users and blocks you place on other users

Swift delivery

  • Your device's real-time location while actively using the Swift runner map or tracking a delivery, so pickup/drop-off and live tracking work
  • Delivery PINs and order status

Notifications

  • A push-notification subscription token for your device/browser (endpoint + encryption keys), if you enable push notifications

Device & usage data

  • Basic device/browser information, app version, and crash/error logs for debugging
  • Standard web log data (IP address, timestamps) collected by our hosting provider

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and verify you're a real student
  • To operate the marketplace: listings, chat between buyers/sellers, escrow payments, and Swift delivery
  • To send you notifications about messages, sales, orders, and Swift jobs — in-app, by email, and as device push notifications
  • To calculate and pay out ambassador commissions and runner/seller earnings
  • To review reports, enforce blocks, and moderate the platform for safety
  • To improve the app, fix bugs, and prevent fraud or abuse

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Camplugie, our users, or the public.

Your choices & rights

  • Notifications: turn push notifications on/off anytime in Settings.
  • Blocking & reporting: block a user to stop them messaging or calling you; report a user to send their conduct to our support team for review.
  • Access & correction: edit your profile and listings directly in the app.
  • Delete your account: available in Settings → Account actions. This removes your profile and personal data from active use; some records (e.g. completed transactions) may be retained as required for accounting, fraud-prevention, or legal reasons.
  • Contact us at support@camplugie.com for any privacy request.

Data retention & security

We retain your data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and access to our database is protected by row-level security so users can only read data they're authorized to see. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Location data

Location is only collected while you actively use Swift delivery features (as a buyer tracking an order, or a runner fulfilling one). We do not track your location in the background or outside of an active Swift session.

Children's privacy

Camplugie is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the "Effective date" above and, for material changes, notify you in-app or by email.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support@camplugie.com.
