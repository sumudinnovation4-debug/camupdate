Child Safety Standards

Effective date: October 15, 2026 · Our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation.

Our commitment

Alphastack Digital Solutions Ltd has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and any content or behaviour that sexualises or endangers children. These are our published child-safety standards for Camplugie.

Our standards

  • Camplugie is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to hold an account.
  • CSAE and CSAM are strictly banned everywhere on Camplugie — in profiles, listings, posts, chats, groups, calls and lectures — and this is stated in our Terms and Community Guidelines.
  • We remove such content, permanently ban the people responsible, and preserve relevant information so it can be handed to the authorities.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement, and report CSAE to the relevant authorities, including the Nigeria Police Force and, where appropriate, international child-protection bodies such as NCMEC, as required by applicable law.
  • Our team reviews reports of child-safety concerns as a priority.

How to report

  • In the app: open the person’s chat → menu → Report user. You can also Block them.
  • By email: info@camplugie.com — use the subject “Child safety”.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, contact the Nigeria Police Force or your local emergency services first.

Compliance with the law

We comply with applicable child-safety laws in Nigeria and the countries where Camplugie is available, including laws on reporting child sexual abuse material.
